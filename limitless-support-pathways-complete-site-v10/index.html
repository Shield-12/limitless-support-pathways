A DIFFERENT KIND OF SUPPORT

Care should feel human.

Every person has a life, not a case file. We begin with the individual, their routines, their strengths, and the people who know them best.

Limitless Support Pathways LLC is designed to support individuals with developmental and intellectual disabilities, including autism, Down syndrome, learning disabilities, and related support needs.

Our goal is simple: help make everyday life safer, steadier, and more manageable. Depending on the person’s needs and the organization’s authorized service scope, support may include community-home care, assistance with daily routines, transportation to medical appointments, and appropriate medication-related assistance such as pharmacy pickup and coordination.

You can love someone deeply and still need help carrying the load.

Families often coordinate appointments, transportation, medications, routines, safety, communication, and countless small details. A strong care partner should help shoulder appropriate responsibilities without taking the family out of the picture.

FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Simple answers to common questions.

If your situation is more specific, contact us and we can talk it through.

Who does Limitless Support Pathways serve?

The website is designed around support for people with developmental and intellectual disabilities, including autism, Down syndrome, learning disabilities, and related needs. Exact eligibility should be confirmed during intake.

Can you provide transportation to medical appointments?

Medical transportation is one of the service areas presented in this concept. Availability, geography, scheduling, and any payer authorization requirements should be confirmed before service begins.

Do you help with medications?

The site describes limited medication-related assistance such as pharmacy pickup and coordination. Any administration, handling, or clinical medication service must remain within the organization’s legal, licensed, and authorized scope.

Do you work with insurance?

The site identifies UnitedHealthcare, BlueCross BlueShield of Tennessee, Cigna Healthcare, Medicare, and TennCare as plans or programs families may ask about. Coverage, network participation, authorization, and eligibility must be verified.
